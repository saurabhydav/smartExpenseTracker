# 🎨 3D Pet Model Delivery Specification & Creative Briefs

## 📐 Technical Requirements (Strict Budget)
- **Format**: `.glb` (binary glTF) with embedded textures and armature rigging.
- **Polycount**: Max 8,000 triangles (4,000 polygons) per creature.
- **Textures**: Single 1024×1024 diffuse map (PBR roughness/metalness optional). Max total asset size **< 5MB**.
- **Rigging & Skeleton**: Standard bone hierarchy with named attachment nodes:
  - `head_top` (for crowns, visors, halos)
  - `chest` (for armor, scarves, collars)
  - `back` (for wings)
  - `right_hand` (for staff / bamboo branch)
- **Required Animations** (Named Clips):
  - `idle` (60FPS seamless looping idle animation with signature quirk)
  - `happy` (bounce / cheer action)
  - `sad` (droop / sleepy action)
  - `walk` (hop / stride in place)
  - `transition` (evolution flare pose)

## 🦁 10 Species Creative Briefs

### 1. 🐱 Milo (Cat)
- **Archetype**: Nimble, elegant companion.
- **Silhouette**: Slim torso, pointed ears, 3-segment curved tail, whiskers.
- **Accessory**: Red neck collar with shiny golden bell.
- **Signature Idle**: Tail flick and ear twitch.

### 2. 🐶 Buddy (Dog)
- **Archetype**: Cheerful, loyal protector.
- **Silhouette**: Stocky build, protruding snout, floppy ears, curved wagging tail.
- **Accessory**: Blue collar with gold bone pendant.
- **Signature Idle**: Tail wag and happy tongue pant.

### 3. 🦊 Rusty (Fox)
- **Archetype**: Cunning, celestial spirit.
- **Silhouette**: Sharp angled snout, Kitsune forehead diamond mark, massive bushy tail with white tip.
- **Accessory**: Celestial forehead diamond.
- **Signature Idle**: Ear swivel and tail curl.

### 4. 🐰 Luna (Bunny)
- **Archetype**: Energetic, swift jumper.
- **Silhouette**: Large hind legs, tall upright ears, cotton-ball tail, small buck teeth.
- **Accessory**: Pink ear bow.
- **Signature Idle**: Vertical hop-in-place and nose twitch.

### 5. 🐼 Bao (Panda)
- **Archetype**: Peaceful, zen master.
- **Silhouette**: Round chunky body, black eye patches, two-tone black limbs on white torso.
- **Accessory**: Bamboo leaf held in mouth.
- **Signature Idle**: Gentle side-to-side body sway.

### 6. 🐨 Koko (Koala)
- **Archetype**: Relaxed, sleepy climber.
- **Silhouette**: Oversized leather nose, fluffy ear hair tufts, stocky low body.
- **Accessory**: Tucked cyan eucalyptus sprig behind ear.
- **Signature Idle**: Slow head-doze nod.

### 7. 🦉 Hoot (Owl)
- **Archetype**: Night & Wisdom archetype.
- **Silhouette**: Forward-facing large round eyes, feathered ear-tufts, wings functioning as arms, talon feet.
- **Signature Idle**: 180° head rotation and wing ruffle.

### 8. 🐢 Shelly (Turtle)
- **Archetype**: Patience & Defense archetype.
- **Silhouette**: Domed protective shell, short stubby legs, gentle smiling face.
- **Signature Idle**: Retract head slightly into shell and slow blink.

### 9. 🦔 Quill (Hedgehog)
- **Archetype**: Speed & Agility archetype.
- **Silhouette**: Round spiky back quills, small pointed snout, tiny paws.
- **Signature Idle**: Quick sniff and roll-into-a-ball spin.

### 10. 🦎 Loti (Axolotl)
- **Archetype**: Water & Regeneration archetype.
- **Silhouette**: Frilly external gill fronds (3 on each side), wide cheerful mouth, aquatic tail fin.
- **Signature Idle**: Floating gill frond sway and happy paddle.
